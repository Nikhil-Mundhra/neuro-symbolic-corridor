# Neuro-Symbolic Autonomous Resolution for Multi-Modal Supply Chain Corridors

## Overview
Cross-border trade corridors linking Asia, the GCC, and Europe (like the India-Middle East-Europe Economic Corridor) lose billions annually to port congestion, customs discrepancies, cold-chain failures, and geopolitical rerouting.

Existing "AI logistics" startups predominantly build visibility dashboards that signal disruptions after they have already occurred. Pure LLMs frequently hallucinate regulatory compliance constraints, while traditional Operations Research (e.g., standard MILP solvers) struggle to compute solutions rapidly under real-time dynamic conditions.

## Technical Challenge & Architecture
Build a **Neuro-Symbolic AI engine** integrating large-scale Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) and agentic workflows with hard **Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (MILP) and formal verification solvers**:

- **Predictive Dynamic Graph Modeling:** Continuous-time monitoring and disruption prediction across maritime, rail, road, and air transit networks.
- **Formally Verified Rerouting:** Mathematical proof of optimal rerouting across sea, air, and rail upon disruption (vessel delays, tariff shifts, cold-chain faults).
- **Autonomous Regulatory & Customs Engine:** Multi-lingual compliance reasoning and automated re-clearing of customs documentation.
- **Autonomous Spot-Market Execution:** Real-time freight re-booking and capacity hedging executed within seconds.

## Target Pilot & Go-to-Market
- **Pilot Partners:** AD Ports Group, Etihad Cargo, DP World, regional freight forwarders.
- **Business Model:** Performance-based take-rate on saved demurrage fees, prevented cold-chain spoilage, and freight rebooking arbitrage.
