# Phased Product Roadmap (Nov 2026 – Q4 2027)

## Phase 0: The "Trojan Horse" MVP & Incubator Entry (Nov 13 – Dec 11, 2026)
- **Milestones**: Nov 17 BTI Startup Expo live terminal/dashboard demo; Dec 11 NYUADEIP Priority Deadline submission.
- **Deliverable**: Gulf Corridor Digital Twin (*Nhava Sheva → Salalah/Sohar → Fujairah / Al Wajajah Green Corridor / Khalifa Port / Jebel Ali / Zayed Int'l Airport → Europe*) backed by Go Gateway + FastAPI + Z3 SMT + HiGHS MILP + React/Deck.gl WebGL UI.

## Phase 1: Shadow-Mode Validation & Customs Symbolic Graph (Jan – Feb 2027)
- **Deliverable**: Full Symbolic Regulatory Compiler encoding GCC Unified Customs Law, UAE Federal Tax Authority rules, and UAE–India / UAE–Turkey CEPA schedules into Z3.
- **Pilot Pipeline**: Partner with 2–3 KIZAD/JAFZA freight forwarders to ingest live EDI/BoL webhooks in Shadow Mode and quantify prevented demurrage ($150–$300/day/TEU) vs. manual dispatching.

## Phase 2: Warm-Start Neural Acceleration & Live Pilot (Mar – Apr 2027)
- **Deliverable**: Train PyTorch Geometric (PyG) T-GNN on collected corridor logs, export to ONNX, and prune 90% of MILP binary branching variables for sub-400ms fleet-scale routing.
- **Commercial Transition**: Human-in-the-loop 1-click reroute approval ahead of April 2027 NYUADEIP Pitch Day.

## Phase 3: Enterprise API Integration & Autonomous Arbitrage (May – Dec 2027)
- **Deliverable**: Direct connectors to **Maqta Gateway**, **Dubai Trade**, and **Etihad Cargo**, plus Hub71 onboarding and a 10–15% performance take-rate clearinghouse model.
